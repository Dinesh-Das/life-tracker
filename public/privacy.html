Privacy Policy for LifeTracker

Last updated: May 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to LifeTracker. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

2. Data We Collect

LifeTracker uses Google OAuth to authenticate users. We collect your Google email address and basic profile information (name, avatar).

3. Google API Disclosure (Limited Use)

LifeTracker's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Google Sheets & Drive Integration

LifeTracker requires specific permissions to access your Google Drive to create and manage a spreadsheet named "LifeTracker Data". We ONLY read and write to this specific file. We do not access or sell your personal data.

5. Contact

For questions, please contact the developer via the app landing page.
